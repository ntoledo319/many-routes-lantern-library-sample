Set it up your way

  1. Read the adult instructions before handing over the first clue.
  2. Place the cards at the listed stations. Use the household objects, or write the station names on paper and arrange them on a table.
  3. Read clues aloud together if helpful. Give hints when needed; there is no race.
Designed with roughly 7–10-year-old readers in mind. Adult setup and supervision are required. Reader needs vary; no fixed play duration or child-playtesting claim is made.
What is included?

A standalone two-clue hunt, adult placement instructions, two hints per clue, answers and a short ending. This free activity does not include the full eight-clue pack.
